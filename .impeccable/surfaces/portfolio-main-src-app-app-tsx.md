## Direction contract

Mode: Experience (hiring audience). Build path: code-led (no image generation on this machine).

THESIS: His career drawn as a metro network. Jobs, client work and projects are stations on four colored lines, set on a real 2023 to 2027 time scale. It refuses the category default: dark hero, gradient, project card grid, tech-icon wall.

OWN-WORLD: Beck-style diagram grammar (orthogonal and 45-degree track only, station ticks, white interchange capsules with ink rings, a framed legend, round line bullets S A F R), enamel station signage, the in-car strip map. Cool off-white enamel ground, near-black ink. Full palette, four roles that own whole bands: Software line green, AI line orange, Forward Deployed line blue, Recognition line yellow. Overpass (Highway Gothic lineage) for signs and figures with tabular years; Atkinson Hyperlegible for body.

STORY: In 10 seconds a recruiter reads the big station sign (name, role line), the legend and the Resume sign. In 60 seconds a hiring manager rides the line down the page: each station states what he did, the number, and its source (job or linked repo). The ride ends at the terminus: Resume, Email, GitHub, LinkedIn.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop 1440: sticky header with the Resume sign top right; an ink enamel station sign, about two thirds wide, name at 88px in two lines, line bullets above, the role line below, a four-color stripe under it; the framed legend box to its right (four line toggles); below, the full-width network diagram on a true time scale, year graticule along the bottom, the three lines converging at the Shopify Visual Search interchange, key stations labelled with their headline number. Phone 390: sign on top, legend as one row of bullets, then a vertical strip map of stations with numbers; Resume sign sticky in the header.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: The ride's four-lane track draws itself as you scroll (SVG stroke-dashoffset scrubbed by GSAP ScrollTrigger over inertial Lenis scroll), a train marker rides the draw head, only the current station lights (its sign fills with its line color), and legend toggles highlight one line's stations everywhere (buttons, aria-pressed, keyboard). Motion grammar: one draw-on of the hero network on load, then scroll-scrubbed only; no fades hiding content. Reduced motion: map fully drawn, no train, no smooth scroll, everything visible.

RAISES:
- Memory Quilt: every number sits on its station, which names the job or links the repo.
- Shader Portal: the real technique, scroll-linked path drawing with Lenis, frozen under reduced motion.
- Oscilloscope Bench: dated stations sit on a true time scale; years marked along the track.
- Scene Magazine: line colors own full-bleed bands (Recognition yellow, client work blue, projects orange, terminus green).
- Shareware Installer: one default action, the Resume sign, one tap away on every screen.
- Moon-Shadow Bazaar: only the current station lights; the rest stays calm.

FORM: Transit line diagram, #7 of 7 on the grounded list (the roll's assignment); seed key e6cc7b6a.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
